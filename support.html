Kinfolio Support

Kinfolio keeps portfolios on your iPhone and iPad. You add a buy, a sale, or cash yourself, or you import a Delta CSV. Today’s worth and the chart use delayed public prices. Kinfolio does not connect to a broker.

Contact

Email jeremyjamesgoh@icloud.com. Include the device you are using, whether you are signed into iCloud, and what you expected to happen.

Getting started

Create a portfolio, then add a holding or import a Delta CSV. If the library has no transactions, a made-up portfolio named Sample appears so the chart has something to show. Delete Sample when you want the library empty. It comes back only if every transaction is gone.

iCloud

Sign in to iCloud on the device and allow Kinfolio to use iCloud. Portfolios then sync to your other iPhones and iPads on the same Apple ID. To share one portfolio with a different Apple ID, open it and use Invite. That person gets read and write access.

Lock and prices

Kinfolio locks when you leave the app. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or the device passcode. Prices, exchange rates, and headlines need a network connection and can be delayed.
